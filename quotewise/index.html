Help

How do I play? Every letter of the hidden quote has a number. Solve the clues under the puzzle. Each correct letter fills in everywhere its number appears, until the whole quote is revealed.

I bought Premium or Remove Ads on another iPhone. Open Settings in Quotewise and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID.

How do I cancel my subscription? On your iPhone open Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Quotewise, and tap Cancel. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date.

How do I change my ad privacy choices? In Quotewise, open Settings › Privacy choices for ads.

Contact us: quotewise@quietowl.app. We answer within two business days.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Quotewise does not have accounts and does not ask for your name, email, or contacts. Your puzzle progress, streak, and settings are stored only on your device.

Advertising. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob between puzzles. Google may collect your device’s advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking when asked), your approximate location based on your IP address, and information about the ads you see, in order to show and measure ads. In the European Economic Area, the UK, and some US states, we ask for your consent first, and you can change it at any time in Settings › Privacy choices for ads. Google’s policy: How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases. Purchases are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to check which purchases belong to your device. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app ID and your purchase history, not your name or payment details. RevenueCat’s policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.

Notifications. If you turn on the daily reminder, the notification is scheduled on your device. No data is sent to us.

Children. Quotewise is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Deleting your data. Use Settings › Erase my progress, or delete the app. To reset your advertising identifier, use iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Changes. If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
