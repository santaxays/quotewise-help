Help

How do we connect? One of you taps Get my code and sends it. The other types the code on the same screen. Codes work for 24 hours.

Why can't I see my partner's answer? Answers are sealed until you have both answered today's question. Write yours and both open at once.

We missed a day. Open the Us tab. If only yesterday was missed, you can save your streak with a free saver or a short video.

Notifications. Turn them on in Hearthside › Us › Settings, or in iPhone Settings › Notifications › Hearthside.

How do I disconnect or delete my account? In the Us tab, tap Disconnect or Delete my account and data. Both take effect immediately.

Contact us: hearthside@quietowl.app. We answer within two business days.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Hearthside lets two partners answer a daily question together. We built it to collect as little as possible: no email, no phone number, no password and no contacts.

What we store. When you start, we create an anonymous account with a random ID and the first name you type. To run the shared space we store: your partner link, your time zone, your answers to the daily question and the guessing game, reactions and replies, your mood check-in, your relationship stage and, if you enter it, the month you got together. If you allow notifications, we store your device's push token so we can tell you when your partner answers.

Who can see it. Only you and the partner you connected with. Your partner sees your answer only after answering too. We do not sell your data, and we do not use your answers for advertising.

Where it lives. Data is stored on Cloudflare's servers (Cloudflare Workers and D1) and sent over encrypted connections. Notifications are delivered through Apple Push Notification service.

Advertising. The app shows ads from Google AdMob: a short ad after you finish a deck or leave your history, and optional videos you choose to watch. Ads never appear around the daily question. Google may collect your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking when asked), your approximate location based on your IP address, and information about the ads you see. In the European Economic Area, the UK and some US states we ask for your consent first, and you can change it in Us › Privacy choices for ads. Google's policy: How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Deleting your data. Disconnecting from your partner deletes your shared answers, streak and history for both of you. Delete my account and data deletes your account and everything above right away. Accounts that never connect with a partner are deleted automatically after 60 days without use.

Children. Hearthside is meant for adults in relationships and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes. If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
