Steady

GLP-1 shot, weight and symptom tracker · by Quiet Owl Studio

Help

Steady is a record-keeping app. It does not give medical advice, diagnose, or recommend doses. Always follow your prescriber. If a symptom is severe or does not go away, contact your doctor; severe stomach pain that does not stop needs urgent medical care.

Logging a shot. Tap Log shot on the Today screen, pick your dose and injection site. Steady suggests the site you have not used the longest, to help you rotate.

Reminders. Settings › Shot reminder. Weekly medications get a reminder on shot day and a heads-up the evening before. If reminders do not arrive, check iPhone Settings › Notifications › Steady.

What is the "estimated level" chart? A rough illustration based on the published half-life of your medication and the shots you logged. It is not a measurement and should not be used to make medical decisions.

Moving to a new iPhone. Your data is included in your normal iPhone backup (iCloud or computer) and comes back when you restore it. You can also export a CSV report in Settings.

Contact us: steady@quietowl.app. We answer within two business days.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Steady has no accounts and no servers. Everything you enter (medication, doses, injection sites, weight, height, goals, symptoms, water and protein) is stored only on your iPhone. We never receive it, and we never sell or share it.

Advertising. The free app shows ads from Google AdMob: a short ad when you leave the Progress screen, and optional videos you choose to watch. Ads are non-personalized: we do not ask to track you, and none of your health information is ever sent to Google or any ad network. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect technical data such as your approximate location based on your IP address, device information, and how you interact with ads. In regions where the law requires it, Google asks for your consent first, and you can change it in Settings › Privacy choices for ads. Google's policy: How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Reminders are scheduled on your device. No data is sent to us.

Exported reports are created on your device and shared only where you choose.

Deleting your data. Settings › Erase all data, or delete the app.

Children. Steady is for adults and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes. If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
