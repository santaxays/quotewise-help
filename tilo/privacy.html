Privacy Policy

Tilo · by Quiet Owl Studio · Last updated November 4, 2026

The short version

Tilo is made for parents. Your child's name, progress, feelings check-ins and quest history stay on your phone. We do not show ads, we do not track you across apps or websites, and we never sell data.

On this website

The talent quiz runs entirely in your browser. Your child's name is never sent to us.

If you join the founding families list, we store: your email address, your child's age, the quiz summary (hero class, two strongest branches and one branch to explore), where you came from (for example a link someone shared), and the date. We use this only to tell you when Tilo launches, honor your founding price, and send occasional parenting tips. Every email has an unsubscribe link.

The list is stored on Cloudflare (our hosting provider) in the United States. We keep it until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it, and no longer than 24 months after launch.

We do not use advertising cookies or third-party analytics on this site.

In the Tilo app

Your child's profile (first name, age, avatar), quest history, feelings check-ins and talent tree are stored only on your phone. We cannot see them. Deleting the app deletes them.

To know whether your phone has Premium, the app creates a random code for this installation (it contains nothing about you or your child) and sends it to our server, which answers yes or no. Reminders are scheduled on your phone; nothing is sent to us.

Premium is bought on our website through Paddle, our reseller and merchant of record. Paddle tells us the subscription status, plan, renewal date and the email you used, and which installation started the purchase, so we can unlock Premium on that phone. We never receive your card details.

The app has no ads, no analytics SDKs and no tracking.

Children

Tilo is designed for parents and guardians, not for children to use alone. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The parts of the app a child may tap (games, feelings faces, calm corner) work offline and send nothing to us. The parents' area is behind a grown-up check. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your data at any time by emailing support@quietowl.app. Depending on where you live (for example California or the EU), you may have additional rights, and we honor them for everyone.

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we will update the date above and tell list members by email.

Questions? Write to support@quietowl.app. See also: Privacy · Terms · Refunds
