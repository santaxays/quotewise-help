Refund Policy

Tilo · by Quiet Owl Studio · Last updated November 3, 2026

Free trial first

Premium starts with a 7-day free trial, so you can try every branch before you pay. We remind you a day before the trial ends. Cancel any time before then and you will not be charged.

Bought on our website (Paddle)

If Tilo is not right for your family, you can request a full refund within 14 days of a payment, no questions asked. Reply to your Paddle receipt email or contact Paddle through paddle.net, or email us at support@quietowl.app and we will make sure it is handled. Paddle.com is our reseller and Merchant of Record and processes refunds to your original payment method.

Bought in the app (Apple)

Apple handles all payments and refunds for in-app subscriptions. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com. We cannot issue refunds for App Store purchases ourselves, but we are happy to help if something went wrong.

Cancelling

Cancelling stops future renewals. You keep Premium until the end of the period you paid for, and your child's tree stays on your phone either way.

Questions? Write to support@quietowl.app. See also: Privacy · Terms · Refunds
