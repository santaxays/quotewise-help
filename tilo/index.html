Questions

Good questions from good parents

Is this a test or a diagnosis?

No. Tilo never compares your child with norms or other kids. The quiz is a starting picture from your answers, and it changes as your child plays and grows. If you have concerns about development, talk to your pediatrician.

How much screen time is it?

Almost none for your child. Quests happen off-screen, together with you. The optional games are 3–5 minutes, and only 3 a day earn progress.

What ages is Tilo for?

Ages 2 to 7. Quests are matched to your child's age, and branches unlock new skills as they grow.

What data do you collect?

Your child's name and progress stay on your phone. On this page we only store your email (if you give it) and the quiz summary, so we can tell you when Tilo launches. No ads, no tracking, no selling data. Privacy policy.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time, in two taps. During the 7-day trial you are not charged, and we remind you a day before it ends.
